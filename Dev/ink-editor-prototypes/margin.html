Recording polish

The menu bar stop button should show elapsed time1. Right now it only turns red, so you can't tell how long you have been recording.

When a recording ends, the rename panel opens before the file has finished writing2, and the preview stays blank for a second.

The microphone toggle3 should remember its last state between recordings.

Recordings shorter than one second should be thrown away without asking.

⌘F drops a numbered circle at the cursor and opens its card. Return saves and puts you back in the text. Esc on an empty card removes it. Click a circle to jump to its note.

Sketch on any card opens a small drawing pad. Deleting a circle in the text removes its note; ⌘Z brings both back.
