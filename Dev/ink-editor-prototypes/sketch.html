Capture overlay rework

The size readout sits on top of the selection and hides what you are capturing. Move it outside, next to the corner you are dragging.

When the selection touches a screen edge, flip the readout to the inside corner instead.

Keep the crosshair lines. People use them to line things up.

No modes: type in text, draw in a sketch. Type /sketch on an empty line and press Return, or press ⌘D, to add one. Drag the bar under a sketch to make it taller.

Keys: P pen · H highlighter · E eraser · 1–5 colors (when the text is not focused) · ⌘Z undoes ink.
